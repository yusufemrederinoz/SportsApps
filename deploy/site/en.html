Eight games, one app

All of them are turn-based games for two. Each has its own points and its own jokers.

  1. XOX

    Name a footballer who fits both the row and the column, and line up three cells.

  2. Card Duel

    Pick 7 footballers who fit the theme. The questions are hidden; play one card against your opponent's each round.

  3. Lineup Draft

    Take one player from the club of each round and build the strongest squad for the target stat.

  4. Higher or Lower

    Two footballers, one measure. Pick the higher one and keep your streak.

  5. Club Chain

    Name a footballer who shared a club with the previous one. Break the chain and you lose the round.

  6. Answer Auction

    Say how many answers you can name and raise. If your opponent calls it, you have to name them.

  7. Top Ten

    Take turns finding the names on the list. A name that is not on it costs a life.

  8. Career Path

    Clubs appear in career order. Whoever names the footballer with the fewest clues wins.

Common questions

Is ChallengeGoal free?

Yes, the app is free. Goals, which you spend on jokers during a match, come from playing, the daily reward or watching an ad; you can also buy them if you like.

Which phones does it run on?

Android phones and iPhone. The app is coming to Google Play and the App Store soon.

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes, because matches are played online. XOX can also be played against the bot without a connection.

Why are there no club logos?

ChallengeGoal is not affiliated with any club, league or federation, so we do not use official logos or kits. The footballer portraits are illustrations.

How do I delete my account?

Use "Delete account" on the account screen in the app, or follow the steps on the account deletion page (in Turkish). Your data is deleted with your account.

Something wrong?

Write to us about bugs, ideas or anything to do with your account. Turkish or English is fine.
